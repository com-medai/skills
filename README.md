### How to Use MED-AI Skills

MED-AI skills are reusable instructions that guide your AI tool through research tasks like searching the literature, preparing an IRB application, or checking your manuscript’s references.

1. **Choose a skill.** Download the skill for the task you’re working on.
2. **Add it to your AI tool.** Follow the setup guide for your platform. Once the skill is saved, you can use it again whenever you need it.
3. **Make it your own.** Edit the instructions to fit your project or workflow, then save your updated version for future use.

Don’t be intimidated. You’ve got this!
